# Integration

Each must-have from `ganger/brain/CONSTITUTION.md`, with the file and function that implements it. Every screen reads and writes the Dexie database in `src/db/schema.ts` (through `src/lib/store.ts` or the table itself); no screen keeps its own copy of stored data. Every screen is reached from the sidebar in `src/App.tsx`, which is only shown once a workspace has been chosen on the first screen. The same list, in machine-readable form, is `ganger/reports/coverage.json`.

## How the pieces connect
- **Workspaces (pen names):** the first screen (`WorkspaceGate`) offers "New Workspace" and the workspaces already saved on this computer. "New Workspace" opens the create menu: type a pen name, or "Generate a pen name", which makes a workspace with no name yet and opens the Bookshelf on the pen name generator. Every section (Bookshelf, Roadmap, Universe, Write, Edit, Format, Book Cover, Export, Translate, Settings) is nested inside the workspace. The "Workspaces" button at the bottom of the left menu (`WorkspaceSwitcher`, above Settings) switches workspace or adds one. The left menu collapses to icons: the chevron at the top of the menu (`src/App.tsx`, with `SIDEBAR_KEY` remembering the choice) hides the labels and keeps the icons, and `Brand` shows the book mark alone.
- **Pen names and mockups:** in the new-manuscript menu the pen name generator depends on the genre chosen there (`nameStyleFor` in `src/lib/penNames.ts` picks the style of names). It gives a brief list, each shown on a cover mockup in the genre's look, and the author can type a name instead, which also gets a mockup. Choosing a name sets the workspace's pen name. The same picker is on the Book Cover screen. The "Generate pen names" button stays grey until a genre is chosen. When the author has an image AI key, each generated name is drawn as a cover mockup by that model (`PenNamePicker` calls `generateCoverImage` in `src/lib/imageAi.ts`, one picture at a time, with a "Draw cover" button for any one that failed); without a key the drawn mockup from `CoverMockup` is shown. Every cover mockup or picture opens full size when clicked (`ZoomView` and `CoverImage` in `src/components/CoverMockup.tsx`).
- **Bookshelf:** the bookshelf is the main screen. `src/screens/Manuscripts.tsx` draws it as a realistic wooden bookshelf (the `Shelf` planks and the book's own colours from `mockupLook`): every saved or imported book stands on it as a `Spine` with its own height, thickness (from its word count) and lean, series grouped under their name (Book 1, Book 2 ...). Selecting a book plays the pull-off-the-shelf animation (`book-pull` in `src/index.css`), presents its cover (`cover-present`) and floats the manuscript's card info in a card on the cover's right-hand side (`BookPresentation`, with `TitleEditor` and `ManuscriptOptions`). With no books saved or imported, the options "+ New manuscript" and "Import manuscript" sit in a translucent menu at the centre of the bookshelf (`EmptyBookshelf`); they also live in the header, beside "About this author" (`WorkspaceForm`). Nothing but the Bookshelf and Settings is open until the author clicks "Bring to workspace", which saves the previous book first (`selectManuscript` and the Write autosave flusher in `src/state/AppState.tsx`); only then do Roadmap to Translate unlock. "New manuscript" (with its type, the genre guide and the pen name) and "Import manuscript" (`planImport` and `importManuscript` in `src/lib/importManuscript.ts`, reading .txt, .md, .html, .docx or .epub) both open the new manuscript on the Roadmap.
- **Types and series:** `ManuscriptType` is short story, novella, novel or series (`manuscripts.type`; a book made before types is a novel). A series is a container row (type `series`, holds no text) and each of its books is a manuscript with `seriesId` and `volumeNumber`. The Outline screen shows the series' books as a choice, and the Write, Edit, Format, Book Cover and Export screens work on whichever book is chosen, as they do for any book.
- **Roadmap:** `src/screens/Roadmap.tsx` shows a Kanban board of cards from outline to publish (`src/lib/roadmap.ts`, `roadmapCards`). Each step is ticked with its checkbox. The outline steps are optional (`optional` on the card) and do not hold up the stage. Ticking a required step calls `setRoadmapCardDone`, which moves the book's stage on through `advanceStage` (`stageFor` decides the stage from the ticked steps). The stage only moves forward and is not set by hand. The Write column shows the word-count goal (the book's `targetWordCount`) beside the words written so far.
- **Genre guide:** "New manuscript" asks for a title and a genre (Fiction or Non-fiction, then a genre with its guide). The form is laid out as Title beside Type, the Genre dropdown under Title, and the Fiction / Non-fiction buttons under Type (`GenrePicker` returns those cells for the form's grid). The genre sets the word-count goal shown as a preview in the form (`goalWordCount`, which changes with the genre) and the keywords, and can start the outline with its structure (`applyGenreStructure`). The AI staff are told the genre through `genreContext`.
- **Inception lock:** once a book is created, its type, word-count goal, genre and stage are fixed (`patchManuscript`). The only change is a genre mash-up: a second genre from the same fiction or non-fiction list, chosen in `ManuscriptOptions` with `BlendPicker`, stored as `blendGenre`.
- **Book Cover:** the author's image key is separate from the text AI key. `BookCover` builds the prompt from the brief, asks the image model for 1 to 3 pictures, stores each in `coverConcepts`, and "Use as cover" puts one on the manuscript so Export includes it. "The whole jacket" shows the back cover with the description, the spine, and the front cover side by side (`BookJacket` in `src/components/CoverMockup.tsx`).
- **Story info (Outline):** the "Story info" button in the Outline header opens a panel on the right (`StoryInfoPanel`, `src/components/StoryInfoPanel.tsx`) with three lists: characters, locations and situations. Entries are saved per book with `createStoryEntry`, `patchStoryEntry` and `deleteStoryEntry` in `src/lib/storyInfo.ts`, in the `storyEntries` table (schema version 6). The Outliner panel and the Story info panel share the right-hand side, so only one is open at a time. The outliner AI is given the story info as text (`storyInfoToText`). A book's story info is deleted with the book (`deleteManuscript`) and is included in backups.
- **Scenes:** each chapter is a stack of scenes stored inside the chapter HTML as `<section data-segment>` blocks, so revisions, suggestions, the preview, the epub and the Word draft all use the one stored text.
- **Editing workflow:** progress (`editWorkflow`) is stored on the manuscript; each pass can use a different model, chosen in the Edit screen and stored per provider in `settings` (`editModels`).
- **Outline to Write:** "Start writing this" (`chapterForOutlineItem`) and "Create chapters from outline" (`chaptersFromOutline`, in outline order) make `chapters` rows and link them to the outline item through `chapterId`.
- **Format to Export:** The Format screen stores `FormatSettings` on the manuscript. The live preview and the epub both use `buildCss` and the same chapter markup, so the preview matches the exported file. An empty scene break ("None") writes no mark between scenes.
- **Export:** `buildEpub` produces an EPUB 3 file (table of contents, cover, title page, copyright page, KDP metadata) that the author uploads to Amazon KDP. `buildDraftDocx` produces a double-spaced Word file with a title page, a page per chapter, scene breaks and page numbers, for a human editor.
- **Ghostwriter:** the "Ghostwriter" button in the Write header opens its writing space beside the author's (`src/components/Ghostwriter.tsx`). The author's scenes are mirrored into it read-only as they are typed (`MirroredScene`), and it has read-only context from the whole working manuscript, the research items marked "The Ghostwriter can use this" (`researchContext`), the Universe (characters, places and situations) and the outline. Prompted in its text box, the Ghostwriter types its answer straight into the mirrored space, highlighted (the dashed `GhostBlock`), so the change is seen as it happens; once the writing is complete, a check box appears at the top right of the space and ticking it pushes the highlighted text into the author's scene (`onAppend` in `Write.tsx`). The conversation is kept in `agentMessages` per book.
- **Researcher:** the Researcher panel at the top of the research repository takes a topic. `runResearcher` (`src/lib/researcher.ts`) gives the book's genre, description and opening to the `researcher` agent and saves the finished notes as a research item, which the Ghostwriter and the author can then use.
- **Manuscript status:** the Write header reads the status from the editing passes, never from a dropdown. `manuscriptPhase` (`src/lib/editing.ts`) says Draft until the developmental pass is finished, Revision after it, and Finalized once developmental, line, copy and proofreading are all complete.
- **Universe:** the Universe tab is a wiki-style second brain of nodes for characters, locations, situations, concepts, items, factions and events. Each node has free tags that colour it (`tagStyle`, `nodeColor` in `src/lib/universe.ts`), an expandable set of labelled fields (a character starts with personality type, clothing preferences, handicaps and more) and links to other nodes. Character and location nodes keep a space for an artistic rendering (`NodeArtwork` in `src/screens/Universe.tsx`): a portrait for a character, a landscape for a location, uploaded or painted by the image AI (`nodeArtworkPrompt` in `src/lib/universe.ts`, `generateCoverImage` in `src/lib/imageAi.ts`), stored as `universeNodes.image`. "Generate outline from Universe" (`outlineFromUniverse`) hands the whole Universe to the Outliner and adds the returned tree to the outline.
- **AI staff and keys:** `src/lib/ai.ts` (`askAi`, `requestStageReview`, `requestFormatPreset`) calls the provider the author picks in `src/screens/Settings.tsx`: OpenAI, Anthropic, Hugging Face (its OpenAI-compatible router) or Ollama on this computer (`src/lib/providers.ts`). Keys are encrypted by `src/lib/keyVault.ts` (AES-GCM under a non-extractable key kept on this computer). Settings lists them with "Use this key", "Revoke" and "Delete". Ollama needs no key, only a server address. Every model is picked from a dropdown (`ModelSelect`) prefilled with that provider's models (`MODEL_SUGGESTIONS` and `IMAGE_MODEL_SUGGESTIONS`); the model ids are written in `src/lib/providers.ts` and `src/lib/imageAi.ts` only.
- **Word lookup:** `src/lib/lookup.ts` uses the free dictionaryapi.dev for definitions and Datamuse for synonyms, antonyms and spelling suggestions. It needs a connection; the card says so when there is none. Nothing is sent until the author clicks "Look up".
- **Backup:** `createBackup` reads every table except the encrypted key list and writes one JSON file (covers as base64). `restoreBackup` replaces the data in one transaction, after `BackupControls` has shown what the file holds and what would be replaced.

- **Translation:** a translation is a second book in the same workspace, linked to the original. Its chapters are laid out as empty pieces to be filled. Asking for a chapter translates just that chapter (text that sits loose in the chapter, outside any paragraph, is wrapped in one first, so the opening sentences are translated too: `wrapLooseText` in `src/lib/translate.ts`); the author reads it beside the original, may ask the AI proofreader for suggestions (in Edit they are accepted or rejected like any other), and approves it. Deleting a book deletes its translations (`deleteManuscript` in `src/db/schema.ts`).
- **Narration:** the "Listen" button in Write reads the open chapter aloud. "This computer's voice" uses the browser's speech and works offline. "AI voice" uses the OpenAI speech model on the author's key. The audiobook in Export uses the same AI voice, one MP3 per chapter.

## Accounts and connection
- No sign-in: nothing in the app asks for an account.
- Offline: all data is local (IndexedDB), and `public/sw.js` lets the built app open with no connection. The AI staff, the Book Cover pictures and the word lookup need a connection (Ollama runs on this computer and does not); their panels turn back on by themselves when the connection returns (`useOnline` in `src/lib/hooks.ts`).

## Settings
The app reads nothing from the environment (`.env.example` says so). The author's AI key and image AI key are entered in the app under Settings and kept encrypted.
